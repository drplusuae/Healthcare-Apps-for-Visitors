# Healthcare-Apps-for-Visitors

Dubai is known for its convenience, smart services, and easy access to healthcare. For visitors, having the right healthcare apps on a smartphone can make it much easier to find medical information, locate [healthcare facilities](https://drplus.ae/doctor-on-call/), manage appointments, and access medical support when needed.

Whether you are visiting Dubai for a holiday, travelling for business, or staying with family, it is useful to prepare for unexpected health concerns. A minor fever, stomach problem, dehydration, allergic reaction, or injury can quickly interrupt your plans. Fortunately, digital [healthcare services](https://drplus.ae/doctor-on-call/)can help visitors find appropriate medical assistance without unnecessary delays.

Dubai has continued to expand its digital healthcare infrastructure. The Dubai Health Authority (DHA), for example, provides digital services for individuals, tourists, residents, and citizens. 

## Why Should Visitors Use Healthcare Apps in Dubai?

When you are in a new city, finding healthcare can sometimes feel confusing. You may not know which clinic is nearby, whether a doctor is available, or how to access a particular service.

Healthcare apps can make these tasks simpler.

Depending on the service, a healthcare app may help you:

* Find healthcare facilities
* Search for medical services
* Manage or request appointments
* Access telemedicine services
* Review available health information
* Locate healthcare providers
* Keep useful medical information in one place
* Connect with healthcare services without visiting a facility immediately

For tourists, this convenience can be particularly valuable because you may be unfamiliar with Dubai's healthcare system.

## DHA Digital Healthcare Services

The Dubai Health Authority offers digital healthcare services for tourists as well as residents and citizens. Its services cover a wide range of healthcare categories, including emergency medicine, laboratory services, family medicine, pediatrics, pharmacy services, health tourism, and home healthcare.

The DHA app has also been developed as a unified digital gateway to various DHA services. According to DHA, the app provides access to information such as previous medical appointments, laboratory results, medications, vaccinations, and other healthcare-related services, subject to the user's eligibility and access. 

For visitors who are eligible to use particular DHA digital services, having the official app available can be a convenient way to interact with Dubai's healthcare system.

## Telemedicine Apps Can Be Helpful for Minor Health Concerns

Not every health problem requires an immediate trip to a hospital.

If you develop a mild illness while staying in a Dubai hotel or apartment, telemedicine can sometimes be a convenient first step. The UAE Government notes that telemedicine services are available through platforms including the Sahatna app and DHA app. 

Telemedicine can be useful for discussing concerns such as:

* Mild cold and flu symptoms
* Headaches
* Minor stomach problems
* Some skin concerns
* Medication-related questions
* General health advice

However, online consultation is not appropriate for every situation. Serious symptoms, significant injuries, breathing difficulties, severe allergic reactions, chest pain, loss of consciousness, or other potentially dangerous conditions require urgent medical attention.

## What About Medical Records?

Digital health records can make healthcare easier, particularly if you have already received medical treatment during your stay.

Dubai's NABIDH health information exchange connects healthcare providers and facilitates the secure exchange of health information between participating healthcare facilities. 

For visitors, it is still wise to keep important personal medical information available separately, especially if you have:

* Regular medications
* Known allergies
* Previous medical conditions
* Recent medical reports
* Recent prescriptions
* Important vaccination information

A simple digital folder containing your essential medical documents can be useful when travelling.

## Download Apps Before You Need Them

One common mistake travellers make is searching for healthcare apps only after becoming sick.

Instead, prepare before your trip.

Before arriving in Dubai, consider:

1. Downloading relevant official healthcare applications.
2. Checking whether registration is required.
3. Saving important healthcare contact numbers.
4. Keeping your passport and insurance information accessible.
5. Saving your hotel's address on your phone.
6. Identifying healthcare facilities close to your accommodation.
7. Keeping a list of your regular medications.
8. Saving a reliable home doctor or doctor-on-call service.

A few minutes of preparation can save considerable stress later.

## Don't Rely Only on Healthcare Apps During an Emergency

Healthcare apps are useful, but they should never replace emergency services when someone is seriously ill or injured.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency in the UAE, the official UAE Government advises calling **998 for an ambulance**. 

For example, seek emergency help immediately for symptoms such as:

* Severe difficulty breathing
* Chest pain or pressure
* Loss of consciousness
* Serious bleeding
* Severe injuries
* Signs of stroke
* Severe allergic reactions
* Sudden serious deterioration

In these situations, don't spend valuable time searching through healthcare apps for an appointment.

## Healthcare Apps and Travel Insurance

If you are travelling to Dubai, check your travel insurance before you arrive.

Your insurance provider may have its own app or digital portal that allows you to:

* Check policy details
* Find participating healthcare providers
* Submit claims
* Access assistance numbers
* Review coverage information

Keep your insurance details on your phone and, ideally, have an offline copy as well.

Remember that healthcare coverage varies between policies. Always check what your particular travel insurance covers before receiving non-emergency treatment.
